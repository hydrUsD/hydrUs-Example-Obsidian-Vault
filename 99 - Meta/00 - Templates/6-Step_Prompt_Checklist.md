# ✅ 6-Step Prompt Checklist

Use this checklist when writing high-quality prompts. Fill in each section and check off when complete.

> ℹ️ **Tip:** Steps at the top are more important (task, context, examples). Bottom ones add refinement.

---

- [ ] <span style="color:#e74c3c;">**[task] Clearly define your end goal**</span>
  _What do you want to achieve?_  
  ✍️:
  - 

---

- [ ] <span style="color:#e67e22;">**[context] Tailor your response**</span>  
  _What background or situation should be considered?_  
  ✍️:
  - 

---

- [ ] <span style="color:#f1c40f;">**[examples] Mimic style, structure, tone**</span>  
  _What should the output resemble?_ (Provide examples if needed)  
  ✍️:
  - 

---

- [ ] <span style="color:#2ecc71;">**[persona] Embrace a specific expertise**</span>  
  _Should the response come from a particular role or POV?_  
  ✍️:
  - 

---

- [ ] <span style="color:#3498db;">**[format] Choose output style (e.g., bullet points)**</span>  
  _What should the format look like?_  
  ✍️:
  - 

---

- [ ] <span style="color:#9b59b6;">**[tone] Add emotional or stylistic context**</span>  
  _How should the response feel?_ (e.g., friendly, professional)  
  ✍️:
  - 

---
